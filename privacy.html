PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Grammer Quest 3D and related games developed by Sarfaraz Ali handle user information.

Our games are designed to provide educational and interactive gameplay without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information.

Information We Collect

The games do not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share personal information from players.

We do not require players to provide their name, email address, phone number, password, location, photographs, contacts, or other personal information in order to play the games.

Game Data

Gameplay progress, settings, scores, and similar information may be stored locally on the player's device when required by the game.

This locally stored information is used only to provide gameplay features such as saving progress and continuing a game.

This information is not intentionally transmitted to or stored on servers operated by the developer.

LAN Multiplayer

Some games may provide multiplayer functionality through a local area network (LAN).

LAN multiplayer allows participating devices on the same local network to communicate with each other for gameplay purposes.

The developer does not intentionally collect or store the local network information used for multiplayer gameplay.

Network information such as local IP addresses may necessarily be used temporarily by the operating system and networking technology to establish communication between devices. Such technical communication is part of the LAN connection and is not intentionally collected or stored by the developer.

Accounts

The games do not require users to create an account or register with the developer.

Advertising

The games do not intentionally collect personal information for targeted advertising.

If advertising or third-party services are added to a future version of a game, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

Analytics and Tracking

The games do not intentionally use analytics or tracking systems to build personal profiles of players.

Third-Party Services

The games are designed to operate without requiring third-party accounts or external online services for normal gameplay.

If a future version uses a third-party service, such as an online platform, analytics provider, advertising service, or multiplayer service, the relevant privacy practices may be described in an updated version of this policy.

Children's Privacy

The games are educational in nature and are intended to provide grammar-learning activities.

We do not intentionally collect personal information from children or require children to submit personal information in order to play.

Data Security

Because the games do not intentionally collect or maintain personal information on developer-operated servers, there is no developer-operated personal information database associated with normal gameplay.

Users should nevertheless keep their devices and operating systems secure and use trusted sources when downloading software.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the games, website, or services change.

The latest version will be made available on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of the games, you can contact the developer by email.
